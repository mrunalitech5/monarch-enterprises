About Monarch Enterprises

Monarch Enterprises is a professionally managed government registered civil construction company based in Thane, Maharashtra. We specialize in government tenders, civil construction, interior fit-outs and turnkey projects.

Since inception, we have executed multiple government and private projects with a strong commitment to quality, timelines and transparency. Our experience spans civil construction, contracting, renovation and interior projects.
